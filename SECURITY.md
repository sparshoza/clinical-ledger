# Security

Clinical Ledger is a demonstration project. It holds only synthetic data generated with
Synthea, and it is not certified for real patient data (it is not HIPAA compliant). Please do
not enter real personal or health information into the live demo.

## Reporting a problem

If you find a way to read or change another visitor's demo ledger, bypass the append-only rules,
or reach anything beyond your own tenant, please report it privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/sparshoza/clinical-ledger/security/advisories/new)
rather than a public issue. Include the steps to reproduce it. You will get a reply within a
week.

Tampering with your own demo ledger through the page's buttons is the point of the demo, not a
vulnerability.
